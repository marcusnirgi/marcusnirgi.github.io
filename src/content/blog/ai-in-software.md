Ever since OpenAI released ChatGPT, AI has been developing rapidly, and one of the biggest use cases for this has been in coding. At first, it started off as autocomplete extensions for VS Code, but over the years it has quickly evolved into standalone tools working on massive codebases autonomously.

These days you can open up whatever harness you want, whether that is Antigravity, OpenCode, Claude Code, or DeepSeek, and ask for the following:

> "Make me an app to manage my staff, make no mistakes."

You will get your app, but you will pay for it in three different ways:

- **Money**: Most frontier models are priced around $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.
- **Time**: Sure, you would be spending time regardless of whether you write the code or let an agent do it, but now you will forever be chasing bugs, detached from the code, unsure of why every line was written.
- **Skill**: You lose the ability to write code on your own, which bleeds into your ability to review the code that the LLM produces. You become incapable of distinguishing the inevitable mismatch between what you asked for and what the agent actually produced.

I have fallen victim to this too. I have paid in all three ways to some extent, but the one thing keeping me grounded is my passion to understand it all rather than write desperate "get rich quick" products using what people my age call "vibe coding."

What is worse is that older generations and more naive, less technical heads of companies feel the need to take advantage of it. Paying 1,000 EUR a month for an LLM subscription is cheaper than paying an engineer, right? At first glance, that is absolutely the case! But what happens when something catastrophic happens to the product as a result of piling up technical debt and over-abstraction, alongside a lack of understanding from engineers whose skills have deteriorated? The company pays the ultimate price, and its customers will no longer be able to rely on them.

Humans seek solutions from other humans: it gives a sense of assurance, responsibility, and a promise of quality.

**PS**: I absolutely did use AI in the making of this post. Every single thought here was written by me in lowercase with raw punctuation because I wished to speak my mind and use AI how it is meant to be used: as a tool.
